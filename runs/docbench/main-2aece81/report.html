Speed and scaling

Each point is one document checked in its own worker process (median of the timing repetitions), with labelled values at a fixed density. Times are the ref's own steps: read (classify + extract), detect (rules on the whole text), redact (the attached version). Peak memory is the worker's high-water mark during the check minus its resident size before it (the kernel counter is reset per document). Fits are power laws on the log-log points of fully read documents; b ≈ 1 is linear. Sizes the ref read only in part (its 2 MB text cap, its entry cap) are listed as capped and left out of the fits; "≥" means the budget was not reached at the largest size measured.
